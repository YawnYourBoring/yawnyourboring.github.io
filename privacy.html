Privacy Policy

Quiet Numbers LLC · Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy covers our website and Quiet Numbers Pins, an internal tool that posts pins of our own products to our own Pinterest account. Purchases happen on Etsy, and Etsy's privacy policy covers those.

Who uses Quiet Numbers Pins

Only Quiet Numbers LLC. The tool connects to one Pinterest account, ours (@quietnumbersllc). It is not offered to other Pinterest users, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the tool uses

  • Our own boards: names and IDs, so pins go to the right board.
  • Our own pins: the pins it creates, made from our own product images, titles and descriptions, linking to our Etsy listings.
  • Basic performance numbers for our own pins (for example impressions and clicks), so we can see what works.

The tool does not read, collect or store any other Pinterest user's pins, boards, profile or personal information.

How we store it

The tool's Pinterest keys (its app secret and its access and refresh tokens) are kept only in encrypted secret storage (GitHub Actions secrets), are never shown in code or logs, and are never saved in our project files. Pin text and schedules are kept in our private project files. We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Our website

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics, and it collects no personal information.

Deleting data

We can disconnect the tool at any time by revoking its access in our Pinterest settings. When we do, we delete the stored token. Pins we created stay on our account until we remove them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message Quiet Numbers LLC through our Etsy shop, quietnumbersllc.etsy.com.
